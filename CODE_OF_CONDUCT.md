# Community and content policy

RebbeHub gathers the Torah of the Rebbeim and the works of chassidim. The
way we work together should be worthy of that.

## How we treat each other

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to computers or to
  the material. Every question is welcome.
- Assume good intent. Correct the fact, not the person.
- Keep discussion to the work: the catalog, its sources and the software.
- No insults, harassment, or personal attacks, in any language.
- Keep private matters private. Do not publish anyone's personal details.

## What belongs in the catalog

- **Authentic Chabad material**: the Torah of the Rebbeim, the seforim and
  writings of chassidim, and the printings, scans, texts and recordings of
  them.
- **Described respectfully**: titles and descriptions as they are printed
  and as the community refers to them.
- **With sources**: say where a fact comes from - a printing, a page, a
  recording. Unsourced changes may be sent back.
- **Machine output is labelled** until a person checks it, and is never
  presented as the Rebbe's words before that.
- **Rights respected**: add only what you may share; link to it otherwise.

## When something goes wrong

Report it - with the *Report a problem* button, a GitHub issue, or privately
to a steward. Keepers and stewards may hide comments, send back or revert
changes, and suspend accounts that break this policy. Every such action is
logged and can be appealed to the stewards.
