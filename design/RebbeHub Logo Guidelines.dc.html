Logo guidelinesHow to draw, place and colour the RebbeHub logo. Follow these rules everywhere the logo appears: site header, app icon, favicon, social images and print.
01The parts
R
1w1.25w
The bookA tall cover, 4 wide by 5 high. The outer edge is rounder: corners of 1/3 of the width there, 1/5 on the spine side. In the primary logo the outer edge is the left. A centred Latin R in Frank Ruhl Libre Bold, cap height about 60% of the cover height.
RebbeHub
The name"RebbeHub" in Newsreader SemiBold (600), letter-spacing −1.5%. Always one word, capital R and H. Never set it in another font, and never translate or transliterate it inside the logo.

RebbeHub0.4w
The lockupThe name is 0.83× the book width in font size, with a gap of 0.4× the book width. Centre the name optically on the book: it sits about 0.2em lower than its line box.
